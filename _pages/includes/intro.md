I'm currently pursuing a Ph.D. in **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** at **Westlake University**, in collaboration with **Zhejiang University**, under the supervision of **[Professor Yue Zhang](https://frcchang.github.io/)**. Before starting my Ph.D., I worked as a Research Assistant under Professor Zhang's guidance at Westlake University and the Singapore University of Technology and Design. Prior to my doctoral studies, I completed my Bachelor's and Master's degrees at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA).

My research spans **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and **structured information learning**. I am currently focused on leveraging LLMs to tackle information extraction tasks, which are typically task-specific and highly customized, posing significant challenges for current LLMs. The techniques I explore include **agent systems** and **collaboration** between large and small language models. In addition, I have conducted research in **dialogue systems** and **text-to-SQL tasks**, which involve structured information processing. Beyond this, I am also deeply interested in applying these techniques to vertical domains such as **clinical** and **finance**.
